About Us

Ferrix AI was founded by IT operators who were tired of finding out about outages from angry customers instead of their own tools. We build software that reads infrastructure signals the way an experienced sysadmin would — catching the pattern before it becomes a page at 3 a.m.

Our platform runs quietly in the background of your infrastructure, learning what normal looks like for your systems specifically, then acting the moment something drifts from it. No generic thresholds, no noisy alerts — just software that understands your environment.

Services
